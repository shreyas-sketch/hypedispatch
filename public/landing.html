How it works

Set up a workshop once. The reminders run themselves.

No daily copy-pasting into groups. Your team sets up each run in about two minutes, then checks the log.

Add the landing page

Paste a programme's landing page. Claude reads it once and builds a fact sheet: what attendees learn, who hosts it, the format.

Link a WhatsApp number

Scan a QR code from the phone that manages your communities, the same way you link WhatsApp Web.

Add the workshop run

Pick the date and time, paste the bonus form and Zoom links, and tick the groups that should get the messages.

It sends on schedule

Each day a new message is written, checked and posted at your chosen time. Every send is recorded in the Activity log.

Accuracy & safety

Written by AI, checked before every send

Every message is checked before it goes out. A draft that fails the checks is rewritten, or replaced with a safe template.

Facts only from your page

The workshop name and host come from the programme. Any number, date or weekday that doesn't match the fact sheet gets the draft rejected.

No selling to people who joined

Prices and "register now" calls to action are blocked. Links are added by the system, never written by the AI.

Never sent twice

A group gets each type of message at most once a day, even if two workshops point at it. A second copy of the app sending from the same number is flagged in the log.

Quiet at night

No automatic reminders after 10 PM. A message missed because the server was down is skipped, not sent at midnight.

Retries that are careful

Failed groups are retried every 5 minutes for about an hour. If WhatsApp may already have delivered a message, it is flagged for you instead of being resent.

Private by default

The dashboard is behind a team password, and your data stays on your own server. Read how data is handled.

FAQ

Questions teams ask

Who is Hype Dispatch for?

Teams that run recurring online workshops and keep each batch of attendees in a WhatsApp community or group. It handles the reminders between joining and the live session.

Does it send messages to individuals?

No. It only posts in the groups and community announcement channels you pick for each workshop, from a WhatsApp number your team links.

Do we have to approve each message?

No. Messages go out on their own at the time you set. You can preview any message in advance, pause a workshop, or change it at any time.

What if the workshop date changes?

Use Reschedule. The groups are told about the new date five minutes after you save it (or straight away with "Announce now"), and the daily reminders continue toward the new date.

Can several workshops share one WhatsApp number?

Yes. One number can send for any number of workshops, and you can link more than one number.

What does each message cost?

Messages are written with Claude through your own Anthropic API key and sent through your linked WhatsApp number. There is no per-message charge from Hype Dispatch.
